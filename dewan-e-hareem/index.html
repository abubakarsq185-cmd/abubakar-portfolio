Our Story

A hall of private audience — reimagined for your celebrations.

Dewan-e-Hareem is a fine-dining, multi-cuisine destination in Kanju, Swat — a place built around one belief: that guests deserve the very best food and a setting worthy of the moment. The name draws on Mughal grandeur, and our event marquee, Dewan-e-Khas, borrows its name from the royal Hall of Private Audience.

Under one roof we bring together a warm, family-friendly restaurant, an elegant marquee for weddings and large gatherings, and Hareem Bakers — our in-house bakery of cakes and fresh baked goods. All of it framed by the scenery of the Swat Valley.

“We take pride in serving the best food to our customers — with a vision of serving the finest variety.”
